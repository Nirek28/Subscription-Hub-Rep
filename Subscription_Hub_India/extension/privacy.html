Browser usage and privacy

Tracking starts only after pairing, choosing subscription websites, granting website access and pressing Start tracking. Pause at any time from the toolbar popup.

The extension checks the foreground tab's hostname locally, browser focus, and system idle state. It uploads your selected subscription ID, a random event ID, timestamps and duration to your Subscription Hub Render API. It does not upload full URLs, titles, page content, passwords, cookies, search terms or unrelated browsing history.

Pending records and the device credential are stored locally in this extension. Pending records are removed after acknowledgement or permanent rejection. Uploads older than 14 days are rejected. The queue holds at most 20,000 intervals; tracking pauses when full. Credentials expire after 30 days.

Saved usage remains in your account until you delete extension history in website Settings. That action also disconnects all linked browsers. Disconnect locally to clear pending data and credentials; revoke the device in website Settings to invalidate its server access. Uninstalling clears extension-local storage, not server history.

This measures approximate foreground browser time. Idle detection can undercount passive video watching. Phone, TV, desktop apps and background playback are not measured. Multiple devices are summed. The extension is a project prototype, not a billing meter.

Before public distribution, the project operator must publish their contact details and privacy policy covering the hosted service and its actual data handling.
